# Skill verbs (generic → specific)

Every skill is one verb applied to one object. Start with the generic verb, then narrow to what this creator actually does. Each verb below gives: what it means, the distinctions that usually matter at intake, the natural output, and how it nests.

| Verb | What it is | Intake distinctions that usually matter | Natural output | Nests into |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **coach** | Help someone reach a goal they do the work for, over time. Questions before answers. | goal, timeline, what they've tried, constraint (time/money/energy) | a plan with next actions, check-ins, progress review | topics: the recurring situations (e.g. interview prep, negotiation, burnout) |
| **mentor** | Share lived experience and opinions with reasons about a path. | where they are, where they want to be, what decision is in front of them | an opinion with reasons, a recommended path, what to ignore | topics: the decisions people bring (switch jobs?, master's?, start a company?) |
| **guide** | Walk someone through a defined process step by step. | which stage they're at, deadline, what's already done | a checklist for the current step, what's next, what to prepare | steps: one file per stage of the process |
| **review** | Evaluate an artifact against a rubric and say how to fix it. | artifact type, audience, what it's for, what they think is weak | scores per criterion with reasons, top 3 fixes, rewritten weakest part | rubrics: one per artifact type (resume, deck, SOP, design doc) |
| **teach** | Transfer a concept or ability with practice and checks. | current level, target level, time per week, how they learn | a curriculum, a drill, a check with a verdict | modules: one per concept in sequence |
| **plan** | Produce a plan around real constraints. | goal, constraints, resources, horizon | the plan in a fixed format, with a "when it goes wrong" section | plan types: one per horizon or context (weekly, travel, 90-day) |
| **assess** | Score a situation, find gaps, say what to do first. | what's being assessed, against what standard, what evidence exists | a scorecard, the top gaps, the first move | criteria: one file per criterion or dimension |
| **advise** | Help make a specific decision. | the decision, the options, the deadline, what they're afraid of | a framework applied, a recommendation, what would change it | decision types: one per recurring decision |
| **draft** | Produce an artifact in the creator's voice on the client's behalf. | purpose, audience, the client's own voice sample, facts to include | the artifact, ready to send, plus what to verify | artifact types: one per kind (outreach email, letter, bio) |
| **research** | Find and qualify options with a reason per option. | criteria, must-haves, deal-breakers, how many | a ranked list with a reason and next step per item, conflicts listed | sources or categories: one per option type |

## Choosing

- If the person does the work and comes back: **coach**. If the creator does the work: **draft** or **plan**.
- If there's a rubric: **review**. If there's a standard to meet: **assess**.
- If it's a path decision: **mentor** (opinion) or **advise** (framework). Mentor is personal; advise is structured.
- If it's a known multi-step process (visa, application, launch): **guide**.

## Naming

`<verb>-<object>` in lowercase-hyphen. Examples: `coach-career-transition`, `review-pitch-deck`, `guide-o1-evidence`, `plan-weekly-meals`, `assess-system-design`, `mentor-first-engineering-job`.
