## Product Vision (from founder)

**Core model: a two-sided marketplace, like a freelancing platform but for AI agents.**

| Side | Role | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| **Creator** | Agent Builder | Builds agents to solve consumer problems. Could also get leads/clients (future plan). |
| **Consumer** | Agent User | Comes to use an agent to solve their problem. Could be *anyone*. |

**Chain of value:**
Creator builds agent → Consumer uses agent → Consumer's problem gets solved

## The Three Consumer Agents (from founder)

### 1. 🚀 The Startup Thinker
**Core problem:** *"I have a startup idea — will people actually want it?"*
**Who:** Early-stage founders, aspiring entrepreneurs
**What they need:** Idea validation, assumption challenging, finding users, planning next steps

### 2. 🌍 Study Abroad Navigator
**Core problem:** *"I want to study abroad — where do I even start?"*
**Who:** Students looking to study abroad
**What they need:** Country/university selection, scholarships, applications, finances, visas, complete journey guidance

### 3. 💼 The Career Launchpad
**Core problem:** *"I want my dream career — am I actually ready?"*
**Who:** Students and graduates
**What they need:** Career goals analysis, skill gap identification, resume/portfolio improvement, job search, interview prep, career roadmap

## Research Findings Summary

### Startup Founder Challenges
- Fundraising difficulty — investors demand real revenue/signals, not just ideas
- "Innovation trap" — building features instead of solving real problems
- Founder bottleneck — wearing all hats leads to decision paralysis
- Confirmation bias in validation — cherry-picking data that confirms beliefs
- "Build-first mistake" — building before verifying the problem exists
- Tools used: Lean Canvas, ABZ Planning, Notion, Slack
- **Gap:** Need for objective, non-salesy validation data; actionable step-by-step playbooks; bridging idea-to-MVP gap

### Study Abroad Challenges
- **Financial toxicity** — #1 barrier, accounting for currency, living costs, insurance
- **Administrative overwhelm** — visa requirements, university portals, deadline tracking
- **Emotional/cultural adjustment** — rarely prepared for
- **"Pipeline fallacy"** — fixating on PR/high-paying jobs as guaranteed outcomes
- Journey: 18-24 month process across research, tests, applications, visas, logistics
- Tools used: Fastweb, Scholarships.com, Google Scholar, Zotero, Reddit forums, LinkedIn
- **Gap:** No integrated all-in-one tracker; lack of emotional/expectation management support

### Career/Graduate Challenges
- Toughest entry-level job market in 5 years
- **Skills gap** — what educators teach vs. what employers need
- **AI displacement fear** — automation of entry-level roles
- **Ghosting** by employers, increasing anxiety
- **Resume/degree ROI skepticism**
- Tools used: LinkedIn, portfolio websites, "master resume" approach
- **Gap:** Institutional misalignment; skills assessment transparency; training disconnect (student → professional)
