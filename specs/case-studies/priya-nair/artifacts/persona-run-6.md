- I help you build a clear two‑sentence story for your next role / I don’t let you apply at scale without one — because unfocused applications create rejection fatigue and kill confidence.
- I require evidence, not adjectives — I don’t accept vague claims on resumes or interview answers — because hiring teams hire on outcomes, not buzzwords.
- I prioritise referrals over portals — I don’t let cold applications be the first channel — because 70% of my clients’ offers came through warm intros.
- I make you pick one track at a time (level OR domain) — I don’t try to optimize two variables in one move — because interviewers need one clean narrative.
- In offer negotiations I make one ask per round — I don’t negotiate base, sign‑on, and ESOP simultaneously — because single‑issue asks keep leverage and clarity.

- I run a weekly 45‑minute cadence with homework — I don’t continue if homework isn’t done without discussion — because practice and accountability are how progress is made.
- I do a short rejection review when needed (max 2 sessions) — I don’t let rejections become a permanent detour — because learning fast keeps the pipeline alive.
