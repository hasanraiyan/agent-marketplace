Approvals Platform — 10‑Minute Case (talking script)

1) Context (≤2 min)
- Who: Finance + Procurement across 3 BUs (~6 teams; 250–300 active users)
- Problem: Average approval cycle ~4 days; requests stalled with unclear ownership; high resubmissions (~12%) due to data errors.
- Constraints: Legacy ERP as system of record; compliance SLAs; limited IT bandwidth; mobile‑first approvers who live in email/WhatsApp.
- Goal: Cut average cycle time and drive SLA compliance/adoption within 6 months; deliver measurable wins without heavy ERP customizations.

2) Decisions (5 min)
A. Role‑based routing with SLAs + auto‑escalation
- Why: Requests stalled due to unclear owner and no timebound triggers.
- What I did: Defined roles, ownership rules, and SLA tiers with finance/proc; implemented auto‑escalation and reminders; added visibility into current owner.
- Trade‑offs: Kept rule set simple for speed; deferred edge‑case conditional routing to v2.

B. ERP integration for auto‑fill/validation
- Why: Resubmissions and back‑and‑forth due to incomplete/invalid data.
- What I did: Read‑only integration to pull vendor/cost‑center data; client‑side validation; guardrails to prevent submission with missing critical fields.
- Trade‑offs: Chose read‑only + validations over write APIs to reduce risk/time; planned write‑backs post‑adoption.

C. Lightweight mobile/notification flow + bottleneck analytics
- Why: Approvers are busy managers; responsiveness depends on timely nudges and clarity.
- What I did: One‑tap approve/annotate from notification/email; daily digest for pending; dashboards surfacing top 3 bottlenecks/month and SLA breaches by team.
- Trade‑offs: Deferred deep custom workflows; focused on first‑session time‑to‑action and visibility.

3) Results (2 min)
- Cycle time: 4 days → ~1 day (≈−75%) within 6 months (MVP in 3 months; org‑wide by month 6).
- Quality/efficiency: Resubmissions/error rate 12% → 3%; ~12–15 minutes saved per request; ≈₹150–₹200 saved/request.
- Reliability: SLA compliance ~90–92% after routing/auto‑escalation; eliminated stalled requests.
- Adoption: ~140 WAU by month 6 across 3 BUs (~6 teams; 250–300 users total).

4) Learnings (1 min)
- Principle: Reduce time‑to‑action for the approver; make the happy path trivial and surface bottlenecks relentlessly.
- Stakeholder lesson: One‑page metric narrative beats feature demos for alignment and rollout.

5) Transfer to target SaaS/workflow orgs (30–45 sec)
- The same levers apply to ITSM/CLM/procurement/AP tools: ownership clarity, validated inputs, and nudged decisions → faster cycle time and adoption.
