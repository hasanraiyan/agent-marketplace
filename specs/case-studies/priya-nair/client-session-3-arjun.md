Given your situation and the goal you've set, we'll focus on transitioning you from a services PM role to a product PM role in a product company. Let's build a concrete plan for this. Here's how we'll proceed:

### **Phase 0: Decide Track and Timeline**
- **Goal**: Commit to transitioning to a product PM role in a product company within 6–8 months.

### **Phase 1: Clarify Your Story and Market Focus (1–2 weeks)**
- **Goal**: Craft a crisp two-sentence story and establish a focused target market.
- **Action Items**:
  - **Develop Two-Sentence Story**: Highlight your transition from a coordination role to one focused on metrics, roadmaps, and outcomes.
  - **Initial Target List**: Identify around 10–15 companies (Bengaluru/remote) that resonate with your goals. Focus on those known for valuing PM skills you want to develop – like user research, roadmapping, or product discovery.

### **Phase 2: Build Your Assets (1–2 weeks)**
- **Goal**: Prepare robust, evidence-based materials for intros and interviews.

### **Phase 3: Pipeline Building (4–8 weeks)**
- **Goal**: Begin receiving steady interview calls.
- **Action Items**:
  - **Daily Outreach**: Reach out to 5 contacts per day for warm intros, using your templates. Track responses and iterate.
  - **Interview Preparation**: Use story banks (STAR method) to prep for both behavioral and product sense interviews.
  - **Mock Interviews**: Schedule practice interviews weekly to refine responses and boost confidence.

### **Phase 4: Secure Offers and Negotiate (2 weeks)**
- **Goal**: Obtain and negotiate an offer.
- **Action Items**:
  - **Negotiate Based on Market Data**: Make clear, single-issue asks to preserve negotiating power.
  - **Trade-offs & Decision Checklist**: Prioritize base, equity, scope, and start date elements that align with your goals.

### **Support and Learning**
- **Weekly Cadence**: Expect accountability calls; review past weeks, set specific weekly goals, and adjust strategies as needed.
- **Rejection Review**: Be ready to iterate (non-emotionally) on rejection feedback within two sessions to realign.
