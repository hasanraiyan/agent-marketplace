1. **The Product Folks** - Known for a growing community in India with accessible workshops and resources.
2. **NextBigWhat** - Offers a Product Management course with a focus on practical skills.
3. **UpGrad** - Provides a Product Management certification program that is more structured.
4. **Scaler** - Offers mentorship-based programs tailored to engineers moving to product roles.
5. **Eximius Ventures** - Occasionally offers PM bootcamps focusing on emerging markets like India.

**Three criteria to choose a program:**
1. **Curriculum relevance**: Check if the program covers key PM skills like user research, roadmap planning, and data analysis.
2. **Mentorship and community**: Look for programs offering direct mentorship or an active community to network and learn from.
3. **Cost and accessibility**: Ensure the program fits your budget and is accessible either online or locally.

**What to build/demonstrate in the next 3 months:**
1. **Projects**: Develop a small project or product prototype. Consider something that solves a real problem or improves an existing process.
2. **Resume bullets**: Focus on quantifiable achievements in any relevant internships or projects, such as "Led a team of 4 to develop a prototype app that increased user engagement by 20%."
3. **Internships**: Seek internships or part-time roles in startups or tech companies to gain practical experience and exposure to product management processes.
