Here's a simplified decision framework and negotiation plan based on your situation:

### Decision Framework

1. **Base vs. ESOPs**: 
   - Base pay is guaranteed and immediate. Lowering it impacts your short-term financial stability.
   - ESOPs often have vesting schedules and uncertain liquidity timelines. Assess the startup’s exit potential and your tolerance for risk.

2. **Consider Runway**: Determine how long you can comfortably live with a lower base. You mentioned a potential base cut, so how does that affect your monthly budget?

3. **Downside Risk**: Weigh job security and market cycles. Startups bring more volatility, while stability can provide long-term compounding growth opportunities.

4. **Likely ESOP Upside**: Estimate the potential upside based on the startup’s growth potential. Do you believe in their mission and the market opportunity?

5. **Liquidity Timing**: Research typical liquidity events for Series B startups and how long you’re likely to stay.

### Negotiation Plan - Script

1. **Ask for More Base**:
   - "After reviewing the offer, I’m very enthusiastic about joining and driving our shared product vision. That said, the base salary is a bit lower than my current compensation. Given market rates for Senior PM roles, could we explore bringing the base closer to ₹60–65 LPA?"

2. **Ask for More Time**:
   - "I'm very interested in the role and the potential it offers. However, due diligence is crucial to both parties; would it be possible to extend the decision timeline by a few more days to make a well-considered choice?"

Each ask should be straightforward and delivered in a timely and confident manner. Silence after each ask is essential for maintaining your negotiating position.
