Template — strict 6 lines

2) Target user: [who + where + how often they face it]
3) M1: [lead metric] from X to Y in [time window]
4) M2: [activation/conversion] from X% to Y% in [time window]
5) M3: [quality/retention/CSAT/NPS] to [target] in [time window]
6) MVP: [scope: A; B; C; out-of-scope: D/E]

Tiny example — 6 lines filled
1) Problem: Students miss seats in popular electives because the portal has no real‑time alerts; they refresh for hours and still lose seats.
2) Target user: Final/pre‑final year students at [College] registering for electives 2x/semester.
3) M1: Time spent during registration from ~45 min to <10 min in next cycle.
4) M2: Alert recipients who successfully enroll from 0% to 60% in first pilot.
5) M3: CSAT for registration from 2.8/5 to 4.2/5 in first post‑launch cycle.
6) MVP: Seat‑change watcher + push/WhatsApp alert; one‑click deeplink to add course; out‑of‑scope: auto‑enroll, cross‑dept overrides, full mobile app.
